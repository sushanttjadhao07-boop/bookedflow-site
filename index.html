FAQ

Questions Diagnostic Labs Ask

What can Booked Flow automate for diagnostic labs?

Patient queries on WhatsApp, test bookings, home sample collection coordination, reminders, staff and technician notifications, and custom workflows such as data entry and follow-ups.

Can patients book tests through WhatsApp?

Yes. Patients message your lab as they normally would. The assistant collects the tests, collection mode, address and preferred time, then confirms the booking.

Can WhatsApp automatically answer test price questions?

Yes, using the price list and test details you provide. Questions it can't answer are passed to your team.

Can you automate home sample collection coordination?

Yes. The request is captured, availability is checked, a slot is coordinated, the technician is notified and the patient receives a confirmation.

Can Booked Flow notify technicians automatically?

Yes. Technicians receive collection details such as test, address and slot without anyone copying and sending them manually.

Can you integrate with our existing tools?

In most cases, yes. We connect WhatsApp, forms, spreadsheets, CRMs, calendars and APIs where required. What's possible depends on your current systems and access.

Can you build custom workflows?

Yes. We design workflows around how your lab already operates, not the other way around.

Will our staff still be able to handle conversations manually?

Yes. Your team can step into any conversation at any time. Automation handles the repetitive parts while people handle exceptions and important decisions.

How does implementation work?

We map your current process, design the workflow, connect the systems involved, and launch. Most labs start with one workflow and expand from there.
