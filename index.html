Frequently Asked Questions

Questions diagnostic labs usually have.

Do we need to replace our existing software?

Not necessarily. BOOKED FLOW is designed to automate specific workflows around the systems your lab already uses. The exact approach depends on your current software and available integrations.

Can you automate home sample collection?

Yes. A workflow can be designed around patient requests, service-area checks, slot selection, technician coordination, notifications and follow-ups.

Can this work with WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp can be used as one of the communication channels for patient enquiries, confirmations, reminders and workflow updates, subject to the capabilities and access available in your WhatsApp setup.

Can the workflow be customized for our lab?

Yes. The workflow should be designed around how your laboratory actually operates, including service areas, collection rules, internal responsibilities and the systems your team uses.

Where should we start?

Start with one repetitive workflow that consumes meaningful staff time. We can map the current process, identify what can be automated and build from there.
